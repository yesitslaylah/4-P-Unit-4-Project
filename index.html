Bored?

Okay. When you want time to kill or just have a nice time out, around the Crown Heights area is where you go! Following is a list of things to do from calm to crazy!

  • Kissa Kissa Jazz Bar - Enjoy a smooth night at this jazz bar
  • Cafe con Libros - Chill, grab a coffee, and read!
  • Bagel Pub - YUM. Delicious bagels is always fun
  • Amani Market - Such a cute little shop for trinkets and gifts
  • The Secret Garden - Beautiful place to go and walk around. See all the beauties of the garden
  • The Brooklyn Museum - The BEST gallery. Time it right, and you'll land a night party event
  • Kick Axe Throwing - Now we're venturing. Throw an axe and let off steam at this place
